About Our Pharmacy

Lifesaver Pharmacy is the premier community pharmacy for the residents of the Kallampara & Feroke , of Kozhikode District in Kerala State.

Our primary focus is on patient care and personalized attention, along with wide range of Skin care and Personal care products.

We aim to provide “hand in hand health care” by creating a personal relationship with patients and their providers by allowing our staff to become a Vital member of your health care team. We strive to provide the best in customer service, quality products, and solutions that are individually customized for you. At Lifesaver Pharmacy, our staff will get to know you by name and give you the personal service you deserve and expect from a community pharmacy.

At Lifesaver Pharmacy, we work with the community and the people in it to give you the best service possible. One of our goals is to provide our patients with quality services to improve their health and make their pharmacy experience more convenient and pleasant.

Our Mission

Lifesaver Pharmacy’s mission is to go above and beyond expectations by improving health care through exceptional service, and patient cantered care to ensure our patients can have a healthier life. We achieve this mission by:

  • Working as a team to thrive in seeing our patients quickly and professionally in a warm welcoming environment.
  • Ensuring our well-trained team is always available to provide exceptional personal patient care.
  • Providing one on one consultations to ensure that patients completely understand their drug regimen.
  • Creating and maintaining personal relationships with doctors, which will enable us to provide excellent and prompt personal care.
