About Our Pharmacy

Lifesaver Pharmacy is the premier community pharmacy for the residents of the Kallampara & Feroke , of Kozhikode District in Kerala State. Our primary focus is on patient care and personalized attention, along with wide range of Skin care and Personal care products. We aim to provide “hand in hand health care” by creating a personal relationship with patients and their providers by allowing our staff to become a Vital member of your health care team. We strive to provide the best in customer service, quality products, and solutions that are individually customized for you. At Lifesaver Pharmacy, our staff will get to know you by name and give you the personal service you deserve and expect from a community pharmacy.

At Remedy Rx Pharmacy, we work with the community and the people in it to give you the best service possible. One of our goals is to provide our patients with quality services to improve their health and make their pharmacy experience more convenient and pleasant.
